# BDC Total Expenses Tagged with OperatingExpenses
Rule ID: DQC.US.0258.10983  
Status: Proposed  
Public Review Release date: TBD  
Effective date: TBD

## Rule Function

**Purpose:**

The purpose of Rule DQC.US.0258 is to ensure that Business Development Companies (BDCs) do not use `OperatingExpenses` for Total expenses before fee waivers or deductions.

**Conditions:**

Assertion 10983 fires when all of the following are true:

1. The filing is a BDC filing (`$BDC_FLAG`).
2. `OperatingExpenses` appears in a statement presentation network ("- Statement -").
3. A fact is reported for `OperatingExpenses`.

The rule reports once per distinct label used for `OperatingExpenses` in each statement.

## Problem Solved by the Rule

The SEC Division of Economic and Risk Analysis (DERA) identified that BDCs incorrectly use `OperatingExpenses` for Total expenses before waivers or deductions. In the US GAAP taxonomy the investment company expense structure is:

```
InvestmentCompanyExpenseAfterReductionOfFeeWaiverAndReimbursement
    = InvestmentIncomeInvestmentExpense
    - InvestmentCompanyContractualAndVoluntaryFeesWaived
    - InvestmentCompanyExpenseOffsetArrangement
    - InvestmentCompanyFeePaidIndirectly
```

| Line item | Element |
|---|---|
| Total expenses before fee waivers and deductions | `InvestmentIncomeInvestmentExpense` |
| Total expenses after fee waivers and reimbursements | `InvestmentCompanyExpenseAfterReductionOfFeeWaiverAndReimbursement` |

**Note:** The proposed rule text referred to `us-gaap:InvestmentCompanyInvestmentExpense`. This element does not exist in the 2026 US GAAP taxonomy. The element that represents total investment expenses before waivers, and that is the parent of the fee waiver calculation, is `InvestmentIncomeInvestmentExpense` ("Amount of expenses related to the generation of investment income").

See: [DERA Data Quality Report – Inappropriate elements used to tag BDC consolidated statements of operations](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-economic-risk-analysis/260827-dqr-inappropriate-elements-used-tag-bdc-consolidated-statements-operations)

## Example Rule Message

The filer is a business development company and has used the element OperatingExpenses with the label "Total expenses before fee waivers" in the statement "0000004 - Statement - Consolidated Statements of Operations". For a business development company total expenses before fee waivers and deductions should be tagged with the element InvestmentIncomeInvestmentExpense. If the line item represents total expenses after fee waivers and reimbursements, the element InvestmentCompanyExpenseAfterReductionOfFeeWaiverAndReimbursement should be used. Please replace the element OperatingExpenses with the appropriate element.
