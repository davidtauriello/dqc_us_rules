# BDC Investment Income Totals Tagged with InvestmentIncomeNet
Rule ID: DQC.US.0257.10982  
Status: Proposed  
Public Review Release date: TBD  
Effective date: TBD

## Rule Function

**Purpose:**

The purpose of Rule DQC.US.0257 is to ensure that Business Development Companies (BDCs) do not use `InvestmentIncomeNet` for Total investment income, Net investment income before tax or Net investment income after tax.

**Conditions:**

Assertion 10982 fires when all of the following are true:

1. The filing is a BDC filing (`$BDC_FLAG`).
2. `InvestmentIncomeNet` appears in a statement presentation network ("- Statement -").
3. A fact is reported for `InvestmentIncomeNet`.

The rule reports once per distinct label used for `InvestmentIncomeNet` in each statement and uses that label to suggest the appropriate element:

| Label contains | Suggested element |
|---|---|
| "total investment income", "gross investment income", or "total" without "net" | `GrossInvestmentIncomeOperating` |
| "before" and "tax"/"excise" | `NetInvestmentIncome` |
| "after" and "tax"/"excise" | `InvestmentIncomeOperatingAfterExpenseAndTax` |
| otherwise | `NetInvestmentIncome` (before tax) or `InvestmentIncomeOperatingAfterExpenseAndTax` (after tax) |

## Problem Solved by the Rule

The SEC Division of Economic and Risk Analysis (DERA) identified that BDCs incorrectly apply `InvestmentIncomeNet` to Total investment income, Net investment income before tax and Net investment income after tax. `InvestmentIncomeNet` is defined as interest and dividend income on **nonoperating** securities after investment expense. The appropriate elements are:

| Line item | Element |
|---|---|
| Total investment income | `GrossInvestmentIncomeOperating` |
| Net investment income before tax | `NetInvestmentIncome` |
| Net investment income after tax | `InvestmentIncomeOperatingAfterExpenseAndTax` |

See: [DERA Data Quality Report – Inappropriate elements used to tag BDC consolidated statements of operations](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-economic-risk-analysis/260827-dqr-inappropriate-elements-used-tag-bdc-consolidated-statements-operations)

## Example Rule Message

The filer is a business development company and has used the element InvestmentIncomeNet with the label "Total investment income" in the statement "0000004 - Statement - Consolidated Statements of Operations". The element InvestmentIncomeNet is defined as interest and dividend income on nonoperating securities after investment expense and is not appropriate for a business development company where investment income is operating income. Based on the label of the line item the appropriate element is GrossInvestmentIncomeOperating.

Rule Element Id: 10982  
Rule version: 32.0.0RC1

## Technical Details

- The label is the label referenced by the preferred label role on the presentation relationship in the statement.
- The rule only evaluates statement networks; use of `InvestmentIncomeNet` only in the notes is not reported.

The rule exists in the 2026 US GAAP taxonomy version.
